Day 0 & The First Spark

Where Every Beautiful Memory Began

Long before the first aarti was sung, our festival began with getting our hands dirty, shopping at dawn, and building a sanctum together.

Long before the dhol beat echoed and the first diya was lit, our festival began with heated WhatsApp discussions, late-night budget pooling, and boundless excitement.

From waking up at 5:00 AM to pick the perfect eco-friendly clay idol, to untangling hundreds of fairy lights, buying marigold garlands by the kilo, and transforming a simple street corner into a divine sanctum — our hands got dirty, but our hearts were completely full.
