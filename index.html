Day 0 & The First Spark

Where Every Beautiful Memory Began

మొదటి పూజకు ముందే మొదలైన మన సందడి, రాత్రంతా ముచ్చట్లు, పందిరి ఏర్పాట్లు ✨ Long before the first aarti was sung, our festival began with getting our hands dirty, shopping at dawn, and building a sanctum together.

Long before the dhol beat echoed and the first diya was lit, our festival began with heated WhatsApp discussions, late-night budget pooling, and boundless excitement.

From waking up at 5:00 AM to pick the perfect eco-friendly clay idol, to untangling hundreds of fairy lights, buying marigold garlands by the kilo, and transforming a simple street corner into a divine sanctum — our hands got dirty, but our hearts were completely full.

Forever Part of Our Memories

“Some people become memories. Some memories become a part of who we are.”

ఎప్పటికీ మన జ్ఞాపకాల్లో నిలిచిపోయే మన ప్రియమైన మిత్రుడు సాయి నిఖిల్ రాజ్ 🙏

Sai Nikhil may no longer be with us, but the moments we shared with him will always remain an inseparable part of who we are.

Every laugh, every celebration, every road trip, and every little memory reminds us that some people leave a permanent place in our hearts.

This celebration is also for the memories we created together. Sai Nikhil, you will always be a part of our story, our brotherhood, and our celebrations.
