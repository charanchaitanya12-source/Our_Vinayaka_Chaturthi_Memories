Day 0 & The First Spark

Where Every Beautiful Memory Began

మొదటి పూజకు ముందే మొదలైన మన సందడి, రాత్రంతా ముచ్చట్లు, పందిరి ఏర్పాట్లు ✨ Long before the first aarti was sung, our festival began with getting our hands dirty, shopping at dawn, and building a sanctum together.

Long before the dhol beat echoed and the first diya was lit, our festival began with heated WhatsApp discussions, late-night budget pooling, and boundless excitement.

From waking up at 5:00 AM to pick the perfect eco-friendly clay idol, to untangling hundreds of fairy lights, buying marigold garlands by the kilo, and transforming a simple street corner into a divine sanctum — our hands got dirty, but our hearts were completely full.

The Road Trip to Bring Bappa

Mandap Setup & Night Prep

Day 0 • Evening & Night

Crafting decorative drapes, hanging glowing fairy lights, sound check, and hours of teamwork.

03

Grand Aagman & Murti Sthapana

Carrying the idol on our shoulders covered in sacred cloth with resonant chants of "Ganpati Bappa Morya".

04

Daily Aartis & Sacred Devotion

Festival Days • Morning & Evening

Camphor flames illuminating Bappa's face, incense smoke, floral offerings, and community prayers.

05

Annadanam Community Feast

06

DJ Night & Celebration Dance

Flashing disco lights, electrifying dhol beats, and dancing non-stop with the gang under the sky.

Visarjan Night • Sacred Immersion

Emotional chants, dancing along the truck, and gently letting Bappa return to the waters with prayers.
